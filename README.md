# Thank you for checking out The Pandora File!

**1. INSTRUCTIONS** <br>
-Download The Pandora File (includes pc & phone version). <br>
-Run the pc version. <br>
-Run the phone version (**Android only**). <br>

**2. CONNECT DEVICES** <br>
-Input the IP shown on the pc version, into the phone version. <br>
-Input the IP shown on the phone version, into the pc version. <br>

**3. PLAY** <br>
-Calibrate your device holding the phone upright with the back facing your screen. <br>

**4. EXTRA** <br>
-When experiencing buggy rotations you can debug by recalibrating your phone. <br>

**5. HAVE FUN!** <br>
